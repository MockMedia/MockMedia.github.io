The Whispering House

You slowly approach the front door and knock three times. Silence. Then, without warning, the door begins to creak open. You peer inside. A single candle rests on a small wooden table near the entrance. Its flame flickers gently, casting long, twisted shadows across the walls.You hesitate. Something doesn't feel right. But curiosity gets the better of you. Taking a deep breath, you step inside. SLAM! The door crashes shut behind you. The door crashes shut behind you. You jump and spin around, reaching for the handle. You pull. Nothing. You try again. LOCKED? Your eyes drift toward the small table. Beside the candle sits a folded piece of paper. You slowly pick it up and unfold it. It reads: "The house knows what you fear. It will show you what you want to see. Not everything is as it seems. Trust your gut... if you can." Sudden chill runs down your spine. From somewhere deep inside the house, you hear a faint whisper. You can’t quite make out the words, you hear footsteps, but can't tell where they are coming from. You feel a cold draft brush against your neck. Off to the left in the distance you see dark room with the door slightly open. To the right, a slight glow comes from a set of stairs.


You hear things. You see things. But something doesn’t feel right. There is only one way out. Where are you going? The dark room, or the glowing stairs?
